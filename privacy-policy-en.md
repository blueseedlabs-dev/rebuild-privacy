# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

"Rebuild" (the "App") is designed with your privacy as our top priority. **The App does not transmit any of your data to anyone.** All data is stored solely on your own device.

## 1. Information the App Handles

The App stores the following information only on your device:

- The promises you create (text) and their start dates
- Streak counts, reset history, and deletion history
- Your birth date, if you choose to provide it (used only for the optional age clock feature)
- Loss amounts or quantities you choose to record
- Display settings such as theme, language, and notifications

This information is **never transmitted to anyone — including us, the developers.** The App does not communicate with any server.

## 2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively in local storage on your own device. The App does not use any external server or cloud service to store your data.

## 3. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account with an email address or password. The App never collects personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

## 4. No Ads, Analytics, or Tracking

The App does not display advertisements. It also does not include any analytics tools that collect usage data, nor any mechanism to track you.

## 5. Sharing Feature

The App lets you share your achievements as an image. Whether to use this feature, and which app you share through (e.g., X, Instagram, email), is **entirely up to you each time.** The App never sends an image anywhere automatically on its own.

## 6. Notifications

If you choose to enable them, the App can send **local notifications** — a device-level feature that does not rely on any server. These include notifications that share a quote and your progress, as well as notifications for custom messages you write yourself, delivered at times you choose. All of these notifications are processed entirely on your device and never pass through an external server. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

## 7. Data Export and Import

The App lets you export your data to a file, and import data from a previously exported file. Where the exported file is saved (on your device, Google Drive, etc.) is **entirely up to you each time.** The App never automatically uploads or sends a file anywhere on its own. Likewise, selecting which file to import is entirely your own action.

## 8. Deleting Your Data

You can fully delete your data at any time using the "Reset All Data" feature in Settings. Uninstalling the App also removes all data stored on your device.

## 9. No Third-Party Sharing

The App does not provide, sell, or share your data with third parties, except when you actively choose to use the sharing or export features described above.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at any specific age group. Because it is designed not to collect personal information such as names or contact details, no personal information belonging to children — or anyone else — is ever transmitted or stored externally.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time in response to legal changes or new App features. We will notify you within the App of any significant changes.
